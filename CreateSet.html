Welcome to Trivia-SmackDown Set Maker!

We assume at this point that you understand how to play Trivia Smackdown and want to asssemble you own Set of Rounds. The content for the game comes from www.EdUGames.com and has been developed over the years by people like you.

The catagories are arranged using The Antoniak Classificaiton System [ACSCoding]
Check the subjects areas you want to see and press List Checked Items. A list box should then contain a list of the Rounds you may want to play in the Set. Holding down the Ctrl Key will allow you to select multiple Lines. When ready to start the Set, complete the Registration Form at the bottom and press "Start Set" Pressing the

Blue First or Nick Name:Zip Code: Highest Grade Completed Play Speed
Red First or Nick Name:Zip Code: Highest Grade Completed Play Speed
You are not required to fill in the Zip and Grade level if you don't want to.
