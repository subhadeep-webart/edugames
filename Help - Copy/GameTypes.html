THE THREE TYPES OF GAMES

Trivia SmackDown is a fast paced two-person knowledge game. There are three types of games and it is important to understand how each works before your start.

Game Type B

Players bid on how many corrects answers the can make. The point award is the square of the number of answers.

Game Type D

Players are asks a queston in which the answer is a number, a date or a location on a map.

Game Type I

Players are asked a question in which an image, music, sound byte or other evidence is slowly presented to help answer the question.
