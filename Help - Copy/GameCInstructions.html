Trivia Game Instructions

In this trivia game, players will drag items from the left side into categories on the right side.

How to Play:

  • Drag items (e.g., names, terms) on the left into their correct categories on the right.

Examples of Challenges:

  • Match National Parks with their states.
  • Match sports terms with their respective sports.
  • Match cities with their countries.

Good luck, and have fun categorizing!
