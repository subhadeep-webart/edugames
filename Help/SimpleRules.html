Some Simple Rules!

This is a two person, fast paced, competitive, knowledge game and it is important to understand a few ground rules before you start.

Keep your hands off the mouse when viewing the challenge and while the other player has the play!!

Two Mice- One Cursor:

The best way to play the game is with each player having a mouse. This is a simple process of getting a second mouse and plugging it into a computer USB port.

An even better way is to also have two cursors using the downlodable program from:

https://www.mousemux.com

It allow each player to have, not only their own cursor, but to have each cursor colored. The program is free for "Two cursors home usage".

Don't Cheat by Stalling

Once a person has the ”Play”, that person must immediately start the play.

Don't cheat by continuing to review the challenges before starting.
