This is a two person, fast paced, competitive, knowledge game and it is important to understand a few ground rules before you start.

Keep your hands off the mouse when viewing the challenge and while the other player has the play!!

Two Mice- One Cursor

The best way to play the game is with each player having their own mouse. This is a simple process of getting a second mouse and plugging it into the computer USB ports or using a multi-plug extension.

Don't stall

Once a person has the ”Play”, that person must immediately start the play and not cheat by continuing to review the challenges before starting the play.



What follows are simple explanations of how each type of Round is played. These instructions are also available from a Help Menu.

Game Type B

Players will be challenged to click-on [or move] a set number of items with the potential score being the square of that number.

Example: 1 = 1, 2 = 4, 3 = 9, 4 = 16 etc.

Game Type B starts with players bidding on how many they can get right. 

The winning bidder will then have a limited amount of time complete the task.

The down side to getting the play is that, if all the specified number of items are not click on [or moved], or any one of them is incorrect, the score goes to the other player.

Game Type D

Players are challenged to select a date, a number or a location on a map or image.

In order to encourage a player to go first, an increasing “Incentive Factor” is displayed. The first player's score will be multiplied by this factor, but only if that player wins.

At any time, a player may elect to go first by saying “I got it!, grabbing the mouse, and pressing the “Press to Go First” button over that player’s name.

Immidiately on completion of the selection of the first player, the Incentive Factor drops to 1.0 and continues incrementing down as the opposing player makes the selection.

Game Type I

Players are challenged to answer a question like “Who is this famous scientist?” and are presented with an increasing number of images, parts of an image, text, audio or video segments that help answer the question.

At any time, a player may elect to answer the question by saying “I got it”, grabbing the mouse and immediately pressing the button that is the first letter of the possible answer. A drop-down menu of all the possible answers that start with that letter is then presented.
