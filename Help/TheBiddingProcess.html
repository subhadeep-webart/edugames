The Bidding Process

Here’s how it works.

Challenge Overview:

Players need to select or move a specific number of items like names, categories or countries, based on the challenge given. Example: Select the seven dwarfs from the list of characters.

Making Bids:

  • Players review the challenge and decide how many they think they do"
    • Player A may start the bid by saying: "I can do three."
    • Player B may respond with a higher bid, like: "I can do five."
    • If Player A is not willing to bid higher than five, then Player B wins the bid
    • If neither Player can continue, then they can agree to press the NEXT ROUND button

    Taking Turns:

    • The player who wins the bid, must immidiately press the corresponding number button above their name, and has the allotted time to complete the challenge.
    • If Player B does it faster than the alloted time, bonus points are awarded.
    • If Player B fails to complete it correctly, Player A receives the points.

    If there are remaining items:

  • On completion of the play, no matter who got the points, the player that lost the bid on the prior play gets to start the bidding on remaining items untill nothing is left, or players agree to select NEXT ROUND.
