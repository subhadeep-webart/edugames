Game D Instructions

In this challenge, players will select a specific date using sliders for the year and month/day.

How to Play:

  • The game will choose which player goes first.
  • While the first player selects their date, the second player must turn their head to avoid seeing the result.
  • After the first player has made their selection, the second player will make their own date selection.

Example Challenge:

When was Pearl Harbor?

Good luck with your date selections!
