Game Overview

Trivia-SmackDown is a two person, fast paced, competitive knowledge game.

Trivia-SmackDown is a two person, fast paced, competitive knowledge game.

Two Mice - Two Cursor

The best way to play the game is with each player having a mouse. This is a simple process of getting a second mouse and plugging it into a computer USB port.

1

Game Type B

Bidding Challenge

Players will be challenged to click-on (or move) a set number of items with the potential score for each number being displayed at the top of the screen.

During the Bidding Round, players review the challenge and decide how many items they believe they can complete correctly. A player can announce their bid, for example, “I can get THREE,” and the opponent can either pass or increase the bid. The bidding continues until one player passes. The player with the highest bid wins the challenge and must immediately select the corresponding bid number, after which the countdown timer begins. The player then has a limited amount of time to correctly select or move the number of items they bid. If the player completes the challenge successfully, they earn the points; however, if they fail to select all required items, select any incorrect item, or run out of time, the points are awarded to the opponent. Bonus points may also be added if the player completes the challenge before the time limit expires.

2

Game Type D

Selection Challenge

Players are challenged to select a date, a number & a location on a map or image

In the Selection Round, the game system determines which player will go first. The selected player makes their choice by selecting the required date, number, or location while the other player must look away from the screen to ensure fair gameplay. Once the first player completes their selection, the turn is passed to the second player, who then makes their own selection without seeing the opponent’s choice. Both selections are then evaluated against the correct answer to determine the score.

3

Game Type I

Bidding Challenge

Players are challenged to answer a question like “Who is this famous scientist?” and are presented with an increasing number of images, parts of an image, text, audio or video segments that help answer the question.

As the clues, images, text, audio, or video segments are gradually revealed, the available Point Award decreases. At any time, a player can choose to answer by saying “I have it” and selecting the button that matches the first letter of the possible answer. The player is then shown a dropdown list containing all possible answers and must select the correct answer within a limited amount of time. If the player fails to find the correct answer, selects an incorrect option, or runs out of time, the Point Award is given to the opponent and the round continues.
