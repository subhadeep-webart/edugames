The Bidding Process

In Trivia-SmackDown, players bid on how many items they can correctly answer or select in each round. Here’s how it works:

Challenge Overview:

Players need to select or move a specific number of items (like names, categories, etc.) based on the challenge given.

Bidding:

Each player looks at the set number of buttons above their name, which represents how many items they could attempt to select. For example, a challenge might be: "Select 7 Democrats from an image of 20 presidents."

Making Bids:

  • Player A can say, "I can do 3."
  • Player B can respond with a higher bid, like "I can do 5."
  • If Player A can't bid higher than 5, then Player B will go first.

Taking Turns:

  • The player who wins the bid (in this case, Player B) presses the button corresponding to their bid (here, "5") and has the allotted time to complete the challenge.
  • If Player B fails to complete it correctly, Player A receives the points.

Bonus Points:

If a player completes their challenge before time runs out, they earn extra bonus points!

This system adds excitement and strategy as players compete to claim the most points! Enjoy your game!
