A Trivia-SmackDown Set is assembled by a Game Master and he/she may designate the amount of Points for each of the Rounds in order to balance the Set. This is done by a Point Factor [Game type B and D] or a starting amount [Game Type I] The default amount is 100 Points, or what ever Point factor is needed to achieve a perfect score = 100 Points.

There are thee types of Games in Trivia SmackDown scored as follows:

Game B

Game type B involves players bidding to see how much of the challenge they can get correct. If they succeed in getting all correct in the allotted time, they get a Point Award that is the square of the number correct, times the Point Factor displayed at the top of the screen, plus a Time Bonus, if they beat the clock.

Point Award = (Number Correct) Squared  X   Point Factor  +   Time Bonus.

However, if they do not beat the clock, or get even one incorrect, the opposing player get a Point Award that is the square of the number of items that the player tried to get correct, times the Point Factor.

Point Award = (Number Correct) Squared  X   Point Factor.

Game D

Game type D requires the players to respond to the challenge with a number, date or location on a map. The Point Award is the ratio of how much closer the closets player is to the correct answer than the other player times the Point Factor.

Tie scores split the Point Award.

In addition to the above, as an incentive to get a player to go first, an additional Incentive Factor is added to the equation as follows:

When the Round starts, an Incentive Factor, starting at 1 and incrementally increasing to 2 is displayed. Whichever player goes first has their score multiplied by the Incentive Factor at the time that player makes the selection, if that player wins.

Example: If the final score is 60 Points and the Incentive Factor is 1.3, the score becomes 78 Points

Point Award= (1 - Ratio(closets distance from correct answer)/(next closets from correct answer)) X Point Factor X Incentive Factor.

For example: If the Point Factor is 100 and the correct answer is 10 with Blue going second and selecting 7 and Red going first and selecting 5, the ratio would be (10 - 7)/(10-5) or 3/5 or 0.6. The Point Award would be (1 - 0.6) X 100 or 40 Points

Game I

Game I involves a Point Award, usually starting at 100 Points, that is decreasing as more information is presented.

If correctly answered

The Point award is what was displayed when the answer was selected.

If incorrectly answered

A fraction of the displayed Point Award goes to the opposing player. The default is 25%, but is up to the Game Master.
