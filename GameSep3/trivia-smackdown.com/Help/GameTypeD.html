GAME TYPE D

THE CHALLENGE

Game Type D asks a question in which the answer is a number, a date or a location on a map.

IMPORTANT: While they review the challenge, each player must keep their hands off the mouse.

THE INCENTIVE TO GO FIRST

In order to induce a player to go first, a Bonus Factor [Displayed at the top] is added to the player that elects to go first.

GOING FIRST:

At any time, a player may announce "I'm going first", immediately grab a mouse, press the "Go First" button and make a selection.

GOING SECOND

Immediately after the first player makes his/her selection the second player then can grab a mouse and makes a selection.

[Things you don't need to know to play, but included if you are interested]

1)The Point Factor displayed at the time of a selection is applied to that player, but only if that player wins.

1)The point factor starts at 1.0 and continues upward to 2.0 and then starts down again.
